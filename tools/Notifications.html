Event Notifications

On this page you can subscribe to receive an email with upcoming events on Plug.Events.

  • Umbrella — filters which events you see. Only events linked to the umbrella you enter will appear in your emails.
  • Every (weeks) and Send on work together to set your schedule. If you choose 2 weeks and Wednesday, you will receive an email every 2 weeks on Wednesday, and each email will list all events taking place in those next 2 weeks. A shorter interval means more frequent emails covering fewer events; a longer interval means less frequent emails covering more events.
  • Location (optional) — adds a driving distance to each event in the email so you can quickly see how far away it is. It does not filter events; you will still see all events for your umbrella regardless of distance. You do not need to enter your exact address — a nearby town or landmark works just as well.
Privacy
When you subscribe we will store your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your latitude and longitude. With every subscription confirmation and in every notification email you will receive a link to unsubscribe, which will permanently remove all your stored data. No data will be shared with third parties. All data is stored in Microsoft Azure within the European Economic Area.
